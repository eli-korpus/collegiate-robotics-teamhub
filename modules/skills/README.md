# Skills & Training

**Purpose:** record proven abilities with a sign-off — “Drill press safety”, “Onshape basics”, “Certified driver”.

- **My skills:** your badges, “Skills you can learn next” (skills whose prerequisites you have), and what comes later.
- **Everyone:** a people × skills matrix, filterable by subteam, with CSV export.
- Sign-offs record who signed and when. Nobody can sign themselves off.
- Skill badges show on profiles, styled differently from position badges.

**Not for:** job titles and responsibilities (Positions in People), tracking work (Tasks).

**Permissions:** manage skills (Mentors), sign off (Mentors, Captains, and the “Safety Captain” position if it exists).
