# Sponsors CRM

**Purpose:** track sponsor relationships and follow-ups.

- **Pipeline:** Prospect → Asked → Committed → Declined / Past, drag to move.
- **Follow-ups:** next steps by date, plus a reminder for committed sponsors who haven't been thanked.
- “What they gave” is free text — there is deliberately no money accounting.
- The whole tab (sidebar item, search, data) is visible to captains and mentors only; contact fields carry a lock.
- CSV export for grant applications and the portfolio.

**Not for:** money accounting.

**Permissions:** view (Captains, Mentors), manage (Captains, Mentors).
