# Attendance

**Purpose:** record who came to practices.

- Captains and mentors take attendance and tick people off on a big, phone-friendly roster ("Mark all present"
  included). With the Calendar tab on, attendance is always taken for a practice or event on the calendar (pick it
  from the list, or press Take attendance on the event): no separate practices to keep in sync.
- **Self check-in** (optional): show a rotating 4-digit code and QR on a screen; members type it or scan it.
- **Hours** (optional, chosen in setup): records check-in and check-out times and totals hours.
- Members see only their own record and percentage. Captains and mentors see a sortable team report with CSV export.
- Only presence is stored (absence is implied) so a full season uses well under 1 MB.

**Not for:** planning who will come (use Polls > availability), scheduling (use Calendar; with Calendar enabled, today's
practices offer a one-tap "Take attendance").

**Permissions:** take attendance (Captains, Mentors), edit past attendance (Mentors), see everyone's attendance
(Captains, Mentors), self check-in (everyone).
