# Media Gallery

**Purpose:** share team photos (compressed) and video links.

- Albums hold photos and links. Photos are resized and converted to WebP (≤ 400 KB, EXIF/location stripped) in the
  browser before upload, and stored in a **private** bucket shown through short-lived signed URLs.
- Max photos per album (default 50, setting). An album can also be just a link to Google Photos / Drive.
- YouTube links show a thumbnail. Delete single photos or whole albums; deleted files are removed from storage by the
  daily cleanup.
- No comments.

**Not for:** file storage or backups, documents (use your Drive link).

**Settings:** members can upload too (off by default), max photos per album.

**Permissions:** upload (Captains, Mentors; Members too with the setting), delete any (Captains, Mentors). Everyone can
delete their own photos.
